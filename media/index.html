Sunday morning

Someone has to click Next all service.

verse-cue does that from the vocal mic.

The idea

Read the slide. Listen. Fire before the last word.

Early beats late. No song files to load.

Three moving parts

Listen

Word times from the vocal feed, a few seconds at a time.

Match

Align those words to the lyric already on screen.

Advance

Hit Next a little before the last word starts.

Instrumental slides

Blank slides wait for voice, then go.

Measured

Bench it on your machine. Publish the timing.

One file of config. One loop at runtime.

Try it

Rehearsal first. Send the metrics back.
